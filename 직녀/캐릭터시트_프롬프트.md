### 나이 가드 (모든 인물 컷에 필수)

```
AGE IS CRITICAL: she is 32 years old. Adult facial proportions - a longer oval face,
NOT a round childlike face. Realistic eye size, NOT enlarged anime eyes. Visible nose
bridge, defined cheekbones, a composed grown woman. She must NEVER look like a teenager
or a doll.
```

새 채팅을 열어도 똑같이 한다 — **채팅 기억이 아니라 첨부가 신분증이다.**
